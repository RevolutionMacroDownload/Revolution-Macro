Frequently asked questions

A few things you might be wondering before you get started.

Is it free?

Yes, and it stays free. Pro is a separate subscription for Red AI and a few power features.

Will I get banned?

Revolution reads your screen and uses your mouse and keyboard, the same way you play. It never injects into Roblox or touches game files, which is what actually gets accounts banned.

Why did my antivirus flag it?

Anything that controls your mouse and keyboard gets flagged eventually, and other macros have the same problem.

I already use another macro. Do I start over?

No. Import a preset and you're gathering in a minute. The meta settings ship by default.
